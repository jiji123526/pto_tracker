Quick guide

A few notes to help you get started.

1. Set your status

Enter your employment type, class, pay frequency, hire date, and starting PTO balances, then select Save status.

2. Check your balance

Your current balance includes completed pay periods and logged leave. Use Balance by date to estimate a future balance.

3. Log planned leave

Select Add leave to record Flexible or Standard PTO. Multi-day leave can use a date range and weekdays-only scheduling.

4. Review the projection

The 52-week projection shows how your Standard, Flexible, and combined balances may change over time.

5. Back up your data

Your data stays in this browser. Use Export JSON to save a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.

PTO caps

Standard PTO rollover cap: 160h. Standard PTO balance cap: 160h. Standard usage cap: no cap.

Flexible PTO rollover cap: 108h. Flexible balance, annual accrual, and usage caps: no cap.

Flexible PTO

Initial annual grant: Class F 10h, Class R 7.5h, Class H 5h. The grant occurs on the hire date and on January 1 in subsequent years.
